You are being asked to participate in a research study. The purpose of this study is to investigate how listeners perceive speech and recognize spoken words with idiosyncratic speaker traits.

What You Will Do

If you decide to participate, you will be asked to listen to everyday words, nonsense words, or sentences. You will be asked to provide a simple response about what you heard or saw, for instance, whether it was a real word or a nonsense word, or which of two words you heard.

You will respond by pressing a button or typing on a keyboard. You will also fill out a questionnaire about your demographic background (gender, age, race and ethnicity, language background, hearing status), and about your experience during the experiment (e.g., which words appeared in the experiment, how many voices you recognized, etc.).

The total time for your participation is expected to be less than 10 minutes. The spoken-word recognition task will take about 6–7 minutes, and the demographic information survey will take about 1–2 minutes.

Risks and Discomforts

The risks of participating in this study include a minimal risk of loss of confidentiality since the study involves the collection of limited demographic information. The investigator will make every attempt to reduce these risks by collecting no direct identifiers (e.g., names or email addresses) and ensuring that demographic information collected is not sufficient to identify individuals.

Benefits

You may not directly benefit from participating in this study. However, the results of this study may contribute to our general knowledge about the mechanisms of speech perception, especially in terms of how human listeners cope with speech variability.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is voluntary. Once the experiment begins, you will not be able to skip questions or go back to review or change previous answers. However, you may discontinue your participation at any time by closing the study window without penalty.

Incentive

The incentive for participating in this study is $3.5 per session, and the incentive is paid if the participant completes the study.

Privacy and Confidentiality

Any information collected from you in this study will be stored in a secure location and will not be shared with anyone who does not have authorization to access the information.

Questions and Concerns

If you have any questions about the research, you may contact Dr. Wei Lai at wei.lai@csulb.edu.

If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, you may contact the CSULB Institutional Review Board (IRB) at IRB@csulb.edu, or call (562) 985-8147. An IRB is a committee that reviews research to ensure that the rights and welfare of research participants are protected.

Authorization

By choosing “I give consent,” you are confirming that you are at least 18 years of age, have read and understood the purpose, procedures, risks, and benefits of this study, and that you voluntarily consent to participate.
